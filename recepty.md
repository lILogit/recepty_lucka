Nutritious, High-Protein Recipes
1. High-Protein Egg & Cottage Cheese Muffins
A soft, portable option high in protein and easy to digest.

Ingredients:

4 large eggs

1/2 cup whole-milk cottage cheese or ricotta

1/4 cup finely shredded cheddar cheese

1/2 cup cooked, finely chopped spinach (squeezed dry)

Salt and mild herbs (e.g., parsley or chives)

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 6-cup muffin tin.

Whisk eggs, cottage cheese, cheddar, cooked spinach, and salt together until blended.

Divide evenly into the muffin tin and bake for 20–22 minutes until set.

Eat 1 muffin per meal/snack, chewing thoroughly.

2. Tender Salmon Patties with Avocado-Greek Yogurt Dip
Combines easily digesitble fish protein with healthy fats.

Ingredients:

Patties: 1 canned salmon (6 oz / 170g), drained; 1 egg; 2 tbsp fine almond flour or oat flour; 1 tbsp olive oil.

Dip: 1/2 ripe avocado mashed with 2 tbsp plain full-fat Greek yogurt and a drop of lemon juice.

Instructions:

In a bowl, flake the salmon and mix with egg and flour until combined.

Shape into 4 small, thin patties.

Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Fry patties for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and firm.

Serve 1 patty topped with a spoonful of avocado dip.

3. Savory Chicken & Rice Velvet Soup (Avgolemono Style)
A soothing, high-calorie, egg-enriched soup that provides moisture without excess free liquid.

Ingredients:

2 cups rich chicken bone broth

1/3 cup cooked white rice (soft/overcooked)

1/2 cup cooked, finely shredded or blended chicken breast

1 egg yolk

1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Instructions:

Bring chicken broth and cooked rice to a gentle simmer in a small saucepan. Add shredded chicken.

In a small bowl, whisk the egg yolk with lemon juice.

Slowly pour 1/4 cup of warm broth into the egg mixture while whisking constantly (tempering).

Stir the tempered egg mixture back into the warm soup over low heat for 1–2 minutes until thickened. Do not boil.
